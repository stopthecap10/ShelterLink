# Language Options Demo Script

## 🎯 **Congressional App Challenge Demo: Multilingual Accessibility**

### **Demo Flow (2-3 minutes)**

#### **1. Introduction (30 seconds)**
- "One of the biggest challenges in serving our community is language barriers"
- "Our app is fully accessible in English, Spanish, and French"
- "Let me show you how this works"

#### **2. Language Toggle Demo (45 seconds)**
- **Navigate to any page** (e.g., Find Shelters)
- **Click the language button** in the top navigation
- **Show the dropdown** with all three languages
- **Switch to Spanish** - "Notice how everything changes instantly"
- **Switch to French** - "Even the AI demo works in French"
- **Switch back to English** - "Seamless experience"

#### **3. Language Options Page (60 seconds)**
- **Navigate to /languages** from the menu
- **Show the beautiful language selection interface**
- **Click on Spanish** - "See how the entire interface adapts"
- **Show the AI Demo in Spanish** - "Even our AI matching works in Spanish"
- **Click on French** - "Complete French translation"
- **Demonstrate emergency resources** in different languages

#### **4. Key Features to Highlight (30 seconds)**
- **Real-time translation** - "No page reload needed"
- **Cultural adaptation** - "Emergency numbers are region-appropriate"
- **Professional terminology** - "Social services language is accurate"
- **Accessibility** - "Works with screen readers in all languages"
