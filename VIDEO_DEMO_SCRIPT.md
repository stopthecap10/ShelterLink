**Visual**: Show the home page with hero section, statistics, and clean design.

## 🎥 Production Tips

### Visual Quality
- **Resolution**: Record in 1080p or higher
- **Frame Rate**: 30fps minimum
- **Aspect Ratio**: 16:9 for YouTube compatibility
- **Screen Recording**: Use OBS Studio or similar for high-quality screen capture

### Audio Quality
- **Microphone**: Use a good quality microphone
- **Environment**: Record in a quiet space
- **Audio Levels**: Keep consistent volume levels
- **Background Music**: Use subtle, non-distracting music

### Presentation Tips
- **Pace**: Speak clearly and at a moderate pace
- **Enthusiasm**: Show passion for the project
- **Clarity**: Explain technical concepts in simple terms
- **Engagement**: Make eye contact with the camera

### Technical Setup
- **Browser**: Use Chrome or Firefox for best compatibility
- **Window Size**: Use a standard browser window size
- **Cursor**: Use a visible cursor for demonstrations
- **Zoom**: Ensure text is readable on screen

### Content Organization
- **Introduction**: Hook the viewer in the first 10 seconds
- **Problem**: Clearly define the problem being solved
- **Solution**: Show how the app addresses the problem
- **Features**: Demonstrate key functionality
- **Impact**: Show real-world impact and results
- **Conclusion**: End with a strong call to action
